## 🏢 Who We Are

**Xcelron Electronics Pvt. Ltd.** is an ISO 9001-certified, R&D-driven deep-tech electronics engineering company based in Lucknow, India. We deliver **end-to-end product engineering** — from silicon-level firmware to cloud-connected IoT ecosystems — for startups, enterprises, and government sectors across 8+ countries.

We don't just build products — we build the intelligence behind them.
